I am a **postdoctoral researcher**, currently at the [KU Leuven Voting & Democracy Research Group](https://soc.kuleuven.be/fsw/english/voting-and-democracy), and from October 2026 at **Maastricht University** as part of their [Disinformation & Democracy Sector Plan](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/node/61288/disinformation-democracy-sector-plan).

In my new role, my research agenda will focus primarily on **disinformation as narratives** (rather than isolated falsehoods), asking how recurring storylines shape political judgement in democracies and what role AI might play in this. **Other research interests** include (affective) polarization, electoral behavior, direct democracy, and political finance. 

I wrote my **PhD on party behavior in referendum campaigns** (read a summary [here](https://plc-journal.eu/article/view/24497)) at the KU Leuven, and my work has been published in [Party Politics](https://doi.org/10.1177/13540688261431115), [European Political Science Review](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1755773926100356), [Journal of European Integration](https://doi.org/10.1080/07036337.2026.2623429), and [Politics](https://doi.org/10.1177/02633957241303708?urlappend=%3Futm_source%3Dresearchgate.net%26utm_medium%3Darticle), among others.

I am mainly a **quants** person, working primarily with R, but also have experience in mixed-methods approaches. I am also a firm believer in **open science** practices and always strive to make my data, code and research as widely available as possible. 

Finally, I am passionate about creating **impact** with my research, having contributed to a policy project on democratic participation as an alumnus of the [European Scholars Programme](https://europaeum.org/scholar/toine-paulissen/) while also writing contributions to the London School of Economics' blog on [European Politics and Policy](https://blogs.lse.ac.uk/europpblog/2022/09/02/how-higher-education-institutions-seek-to-influence-eu-policymaking/), and [UK in a Changing Europe](https://ukandeu.ac.uk/indyref2-rejoin-eu-consider-first-reforming-regulations-on-referendum-campaign-finance/)'s blog.
