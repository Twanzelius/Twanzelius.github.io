## Course Overview

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to comparative politics on a European scale. Students will:

- Develop the analytical framework and empirical skills needed to answer complex questions regarding European politics
- Engage with theory, comparative case studies, data sets, and scholarly debates
- Receive hands-on experience by contributing to their very own comparative politics research project.

## Grading

- Written exam: 50%
- Group Project: 50%
